# Spec: Stylized hard fork of MPFB2

Status: ready to implement. Date: 2026-11-09.

## 1. Goal

Characters come out stylized, in the Overwatch / Fortnite range, and do not land in the uncanny
valley. The polygon count is low enough for games, but the silhouette never shows it.

This is a **hard fork**. Realism is removed, not kept as a mode, and backward compatibility with
MakeHuman / MPFB2 assets, `.mhm` files and targets is **not required**. Loading community
clothing is a nice-to-have, so it is the last and optional phase (§12).

Non-goals:
- toon / cel NPR shading, Shader-to-RGB and outlines
- photoreal anything
- keeping the HM08 base mesh as a user-facing option

## 2. Decisions

All are settled; none are open.

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Scope | Hard fork. Realistic skin, the SSS skin, pores and the photo textures are deleted. |
| D2 | Budget | Body + head **9–11k triangles at LOD0** (target 10k; lowered from 18k on PRXJX2: "far too much geometry on the face"), excluding eyes, teeth, tongue, hair and clothes. |
| D3 | Shading | **Stylized PBR**: a Principled BSDF with a painted, low-frequency albedo, low SSS and a soft rim. It must render in Cycles and EEVEE and export to glTF/FBX with no custom nodes. |
| D4 | Topology | **New hand-built base mesh** (`sty01`) with a new UV layout. HM08 stays in the repo only as a build-time conversion source (§5). |
| D5 | Proportions | Heroic default of about 7 heads tall, with a `Stylization` macro slider from 0 (natural) to 1 (chunky, about 6 heads). |
| D6 | Eyes | Anime-leaning but not full anime (user, PRXJX2): eyeball +22% larger, facial detail carried by the texture rather than the mesh, iris covers about 55% of the visible eye opening, simple shader with a painted catchlight. |
| D7 | Hair | Smooth, chunky clump meshes, generated by extending the existing hair-cards geometry nodes. Solid geometry with no alpha. |
| D8 | LODs | 4 levels, each with half the triangles of the level above (10k → 5k → 2.5k → 1.25k), made at export. |

D5–D7 went to the user as questions and were skipped, so the recommended options were adopted.

## 3. Research basis

### Uncanny valley

The valley comes mostly from **mismatched realism**, not from realism as such:

- **MacDorman & Chattopadhyay 2016** (*Cognition* 146): when facial features differ in how
  realistic they are, the face reads as eerier.
- **MacDorman et al. 2009** (*CHB* 25(3)): enlarged eyes or odd proportions are disturbing on
  photoreal skin, and much less so on stylized skin.
- **Seyama & Nagayama 2007** (*Presence* 16(4)): the valley appeared only when realistic faces
  also had abnormal features, such as enlarged eyes.
- **Zell et al. 2015** ("To Stylize or Not to Stylize?", SIGGRAPH Asia): shape drives how
  realistic a face looks; the material, above all the albedo, drives appeal and eeriness.
  Appeal is highest when material and shape are stylized to a similar degree.

Design rules that follow from this. They are acceptance criteria, not suggestions:

1. **R1:** Skin material is stylized at least as far as the shape is. There is no pore detail,
   no photo texture and no skin micro-normal.
2. **R2:** Eyes, skin and proportions change together. Eye size is never raised without the
   skin and shape stylization.
3. **R3:** One level of detail frequency across the whole character. Hair, eyes, teeth and skin
   share the same painted, low-frequency treatment.
4. **R4:** Silhouette first. Shapes read at a distance; interior detail echoes the silhouette
   shapes (Valve, *Illustrative Rendering in TF2*, SIGGRAPH 2007).

### Budgets

- **Overwatch** (Blizzard tech art, 2016):
  - hero LOD0 is about 30k triangles, including the costume; the cap is soft, up to about 34k
  - 4 LODs, each halving the triangle count
  - about 200–225 bones, about 70 of them in the face
  - at most 4 influences per vertex
  - 2048² texture sets
  - skin uses a painted "blood map" instead of real SSS
- **Fortnite:** no official character figure has been published.

D2 puts the nude body at about a third of an Overwatch hero, which leaves plenty of room for
clothes and hair inside about 30k.

### Shading references

The TF2 paper (half-Lambert, a warped ramp, cool shadows, a Fresnel rim) and Overwatch's packed
PBR sets (colour + AO, normal, spec + roughness, emissive) are the models. The Blender
equivalents are in §7.

## 4. New base mesh `sty01`

### 4.1 Topology

Quad-only, built by hand over HM08 once HM08 has been posed into the stylized default shape
(§6).

Triangle budget at LOD0 (counted with each quad as 2 triangles):

| Region | Tris | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Head and neck | 2,000 | concentric loops around the eyes and mouth, 2 rings each (about 300 of the 2,000 tris for both eyes and the mouth; user, PRXJX2: "fewer vertices, especially around the eyes and mouth"); nasolabial loop; equal loops above and below the eye; mouth bag included |
| Hands, ×2 | 1,600 | 2 loops per knuckle, palm loop, fingernail as a face set only (no geometry step) |
| Torso | 2,400 | pectoral and shoulder loops follow the deltoid; 3 loops each at clavicle and waist |
| Arms | 1,200 | 3 loops each at elbow and wrist |
| Legs | 1,800 | 3 loops each at knee, hip and ankle |
| Feet, ×2 | 1,000 | separate toes are **not** modelled: one mitten-style foot, so the toes read as a single shape |
| **Total** | **10,000** | hard limit 11,000; a CI test enforces it (§11) |

More rules:
- **No triangles or n-gons anywhere.**
- Poles (vertices with 3 or 5 edges) are kept out of deforming areas: eyelids, lips, finger and
  limb joints.
- **The mesh is symmetric in X** to within 1e-5. The mirror table is generated, not authored.
- **Genital geometry is left out.** The underwear area is a smooth Barbie-style shape, as in
  both reference games. HM08's `genitals` targets and the `helper-genital` group are deleted.

### 4.2 "Not visibly low-poly"

- The silhouette loops follow the macro outline: shoulders, the curves of calf and forearm,
  the jaw line and the back of the skull. Density goes where the outline curves, not where
  surface detail sits.
- Smooth shading with **custom split normals transferred from a subdivision-level-2 copy**, which
  hides faceting at the terminator. Blender's Smooth by Angle is not used, because it splits
  normals.
- An anatomy normal map at 2048² is baked from a Multires level-3 sculpt of `sty01`. It carries
  the collarbone, kneecap, knuckle and shoulder-blade forms. **No micro-detail** (R1).
- In Blender, a viewport/render SUBSURF level 1 stays available on the body, as the existing
  modifier code already gives assets. It is off for export.

### 4.3 Helpers and joint cubes

`sty01` copies the HM08 scheme, so the masking and export code keeps working:

- **The body comes first** (vertex indices `0 .. N_body-1`), then the helpers, then the joint
  cubes.
- Helpers: `helper-tights`, `helper-skirt`, `helper-hair`, the eye, teeth and tongue helpers,
  and the eyelash helpers. Each helper is rebuilt at about 50% of HM08's density.
- **Joint cubes:** one 8-vertex cube for each `joint-*` group HM08 has. Each cube is centred on
  the HM08 joint position after the stylized shape is applied, then bound to the body with the
  same binding used for targets (§5), so the cubes follow every target.

### 4.4 UVs

- One UDIM-free 0–1 layout. The head gets 35% of the texel area, the hands 10%, and the rest of
  the body the remainder.
- The seams sit under the arms, on the inner legs and at the back of the skull.
- The old `mpfb_*.jpg` region textures and `data/uv_layers/eyelids_*` are deleted. New
  eyelid UV layers are generated from `sty01`.

### 4.5 Metadata files

Every file is regenerated for `sty01`. HM08 versions move to `tools/hm08/`, which is
build-time only and not shipped.

- `data/3dobjs/base.obj`: this file is replaced. Header `# basemesh sty01`.
- `data/mesh_metadata/basemesh_vertex_groups.json`
- `sty01_config.json`, which replaces `hm08_config.json`
- `sty01.mirror`
- the face↔vertex tables

## 5. HM08 → sty01 conversion pipeline (build-time)

This is the core of the fork. **Nothing is re-sculpted by hand** that a binding can carry over.

### 5.1 Binder

- **Build** `tools/convert/bind_sty01.py`, which runs in Blender in the background.
- For each `sty01` vertex, the binder finds the nearest HM08 body triangle on the HM08 mesh
  posed in the stylized default shape. It stores three vertex indices, barycentric weights and
  a normal offset.
- **This is the MHCLO binding record**, so it reuses the math in
  `entities/clothes/vertexmatch.py` and `ClothesService.fit_clothes_to_human`.
- Output goes to `tools/convert/sty01_from_hm08.binding.json`.

### 5.2 Targets

There are 1,258 `.target.gz` files. For each HM08 target, the `sty01` delta at vertex v is the
barycentric-weighted sum of the HM08 deltas at v's three bound vertices. Deltas below 1e-5 are
dropped, which keeps the files sparse.

- **Script:** `tools/convert/convert_targets.py`. It writes into `data/targets/` and leaves the
  `target.json` structure untouched.
- **Kept:** the macrodetails and every section in `target.json`. The race targets are kept as
  head-shape families (see §6.2).
- **Deleted:** the `genitals` section, `macrodetails` `cupsize` / `firmness` with the
  `breast/female-*cup*` combinatorics (replaced as in §6.2), and `expression/` targets that
  depend on realism. The eyelid and mouth expression targets are converted.
- **The face then gets one hand pass.** Converted eye, mouth and nose targets are reviewed in
  Blender with a fixed checklist: each eyelid target closes the lid with no gap, lips meet, and
  the nostrils do not invert. Targets that fail are fixed by hand. This is expected to be about
  5% of the face targets.

### 5.3 Rigs

`data/rigs/**/rig.*.json` locate joints by strategy:

| Strategy | Count | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| CUBE | 1,235 | No change. The cube names are kept (§4.3). |
| VERTEX | 513 | Remap each HM08 index to the `sty01` vertex nearest to its bound position. |
| MEAN | 198 | Remap each HM08 index to the `sty01` vertex nearest to its bound position. |
| XYZ | 8 | No change. |

- **Script:** `tools/convert/convert_rigs.py`. It also regenerates every rig's
  `default_position`.

### 5.4 Weights

`weights.*.json` files are transferred with Blender's Data Transfer (nearest face interpolated)
from HM08 to `sty01` in the default pose. Each weight set is then normalised to **at most 4
influences** (the Overwatch convention), with weights below 0.01 dropped.

Hand-fixed afterwards in Blender:
- the shoulders, the hips and the fingers
- the toes collapse into a single foot weight (§4.1). The `default_no_toes` rig becomes the
  default, and the `*_toes` rigs are deleted.

### 5.5 Hardcoded HM08 references

These five references must change:

| Location | Change |
|---|---|
| `entities/rig.py:888` | Replace `13380` with a `BODY_VERTEX_COUNT` read from `sty01_config.json`. |
| `services/objectservice.py:927` | Replace `13380` with `BODY_VERTEX_COUNT` from `sty01_config.json`. |
| `entities/clothes/mhclo.py:209` | Point the config filename at `sty01_config.json`. |
| `entities/clothes/mhclo.py:262` | `basemesh hm08` becomes `basemesh sty01`. On load, a `.mhclo` / `.proxy` whose `basemesh` line is not `sty01` is refused with a clear message (or sent to §12 when that exists). |
| `services/targetservice.py:40`, `:567` | `sty01.mirror` |

`grep -rn "13380\|hm08\|HM08" src/` must return nothing outside comments once Phase 2 is done.

## 6. Proportions

### 6.1 Stylized default shape

The default shape is authored as one HM08 target first (`stylized-default`), and then **baked
into `sty01`'s rest shape**. The base mesh *is* the stylized default; it is not a target layered
on top. Scales are relative to the HM08 default adult:

| Feature | Change |
|---|---|
| Head | uniform +3%, cranium shape unchanged: overall size and shape close to HM08 (user, PRXJX2); the stylization comes from the features. Face smoothing stays on the front of the face, so the skull is not reshaped |
| Eyes (sockets and lids) | +22% (D6), with lid loops shaped for the larger eyeball; brought 2.5 mm forward toward the face plane |
| Nose and ears | nose −12% toward the cheek plane, brought 5.5 mm forward down to the nostril base; nostril flare flattened by about 80%; ear concha and folds filled up to the rim (helix kept) |
| Mouth | −10% (lips −15% in depth); lips brought 4.5 mm forward, more than the philtrum and chin, so they keep a step in profile; moved 1.5 mm up toward the nose (shorter philtrum) |
| Jaw | lower face 10% narrower toward a smaller chin |
| Face relief | broad low-pass over the head: lid creases, philtrum, lip volume, nasolabial folds and brow ridge removed; eyes, brows and lips read from the texture |
| Hands | +12% |
| Feet | +10% |
| Neck | +6% thicker |
| Shoulders | +6% wider |
| Waist | −4% |
| Lower leg | +3% longer |
| Overall height | about 7.2 heads tall (head/height 0.1385; the check range 0.135–0.15 holds) |

### 6.2 Macro changes

`data/targets/macrodetails/macro.json` changes:

- **New macro `stylization`, from 0 to 1, default 0.35.**
  - At 0 it applies the `stylized-natural` target (about 7.5 heads, head scale ×1.0).
  - At 1 it applies `stylized-chunky` (about 6 heads, head +20%, hands and feet +25%, limbs
    shorter and thicker).
  - The default of 0.35 lands on the §6.1 shape.
  - Both targets are authored on `sty01` directly.
- **`cupsize` and `firmness` become one `bust` macro from 0 to 1.** It has two endpoint targets
  that are smooth, with no nipple geometry.
- **Race macros (african / asian / caucasian) stay as head-shape families,** with these
  changes:
  - they are relabelled "Face shape A / B / C" in the UI
  - they no longer select a skin texture or tone; skin tone is a separate colour (§7.1)
- **`proportions` (ideal / uncommon) is deleted,** because `stylization` replaces it.
- **Baby:** `baby` age targets are converted, but the age slider floor rises to child (age
  value 0.1875 → the minimum child age). Stylized babies are out of scope.

Changes to `ui/new_human/newhuman/properties/`: `phenotype_stylization.json` is added, and the
cupsize and firmness properties are replaced by `phenotype_bust.json`.

## 7. Materials

All realistic skin paths are deleted:

- **Code:** `ENHANCED`, `ENHANCED_SSS`, `LAYERED`, `MAKESKIN` as a skin type,
  `enhancedskinmaterial.py`, `data/node_trees/enhanced_skin.json`, `NodeWrapperSkin`, the
  `MpfbSkin*` composites, `enhanced_settings.default.json`, and the `enhancedsettings` and
  `makeuppresets` UI.
- **Data:** `data/textures/mpfb_*.jpg` and `sss.png`.

MAKESKIN stays for **reading** `.mhmat` files on clothes only.

What remains is two material builders, both written as **nodemodel v2** wrappers in
`entities/nodemodel/v2/materials/`:

### 7.1 `STYLIZED` skin (`nodewrapperstylizedskin.py`)

A single Principled BSDF, which keeps it exportable. Inputs are exposed on the group:

| Input | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `SkinTone` | sRGB (0.80, 0.60, 0.48) | Base colour. The UI also offers 12 swatches as `data/settings/skin_tones.json`. |
| `BloodMap` texture | `data/textures/sty01_bloodmap.png` (1024²) | Painted grayscale mask for warm areas: cheeks, nose tip, ears, lips, knuckles, elbows, knees, palms and soles. |
| `BloodTint` | (0.85, 0.35, 0.30), strength 0.25 | Mixed into `SkinTone` through `BloodMap`. This replaces SSS for the warm look. |
| `CavityMap` | baked AO from the `sty01` sculpt, 2048² | Multiplied into the albedo at 0.4 and tinted toward `BloodTint`, so cavities read warm, not grey. |
| `Gradient` | vertical darkening of −12% from head to feet | Position-based gradient node in object space. Keeps attention on the face. |
| Roughness | 0.55, face 0.50, lips 0.40 | Set through a painted roughness mask; the range stays 0.4–0.6. |
| Specular IOR Level | 0.35 | Lower than the 0.5 default, which takes the plastic edge off. |
| Subsurface Weight | 0.05, radius (1.0, 0.35, 0.2) × 0.5 cm | Only a little SSS. It may be 0 for game export. |
| Normal | anatomy normal map (§4.2) | No micro-normal, no pore noise (R1). |
| `Makeup` | optional decal texture slot | Lip, liner and blush decals, mixed in sRGB. |

- **Export:** at export, `BloodMap`, `CavityMap`, `Gradient` and `SkinTone` are baked into one
  2048² albedo, so a game engine gets plain PBR maps. This reuses the bake plumbing in
  `services/hairbakeservice.py`.
- **`MaterialService.identify_material`:** it detects this material by the `BloodTint` input.

### 7.2 `STYLIZED_EYES` (`nodewrapperstylizedeyes.py`)

Replaces `procedural_eyes.json` and `eye_settings.default.json`:

- **Sclera:** flat (0.92, 0.90, 0.88), with a soft darker edge. No veins.
- **Iris:** a two-colour radial gradient from `IrisInner` to `IrisOuter`, a dark limbal ring at
  15% width, and a pupil of radius 0.35 of the iris.
- **Catchlight:** a painted elliptical highlight at the upper outer side, emission strength 1.5.
  It reads identically under any lighting; this is the "big painted specular" convention.
- **Shell:** Roughness 0.05 and Coat 0.5 on one opaque mesh. **No transmission shell and no
  alpha blend.**

Eye presets keep the existing preset pattern (`ui/presets/eyesettings`) with a new key set.

### 7.3 Lighting helper

There is an optional "Stylized studio" operator. It adds a key light, a cool fill and a strong
rim light, plus a gradient world.

- **Code:** it reuses the custom scene-light code from the hair-cards work.
- **Purpose:** look development only; nothing depends on it.

## 8. Body parts

All are new `.mhclo` assets authored against `sty01` and shipped in `data/bodyparts/`. They
are no longer pulled from asset packs, so the fork works with nothing installed.

| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Eyes | Sphere with 320 tris per eye and a slight cornea bulge (no separate cornea mesh); `STYLIZED_EYES`. One asset, which replaces HIGHPOLY / LOWPOLY. |
| Eyebrows | A solid mesh strip of about 120 tris per brow, a painted shape and no alpha cards. 6 shapes. |
| Eyelashes | One solid "wing" mesh per lid, about 60 tris, dark colour, no alpha. 2 styles (none / lashed). |
| Teeth | One merged upper and one merged lower strip, about 400 tris in total. No separate tooth geometry. Flat white (0.95, 0.94, 0.90), roughness 0.3. |
| Tongue | About 200 tris, coloured to match `BloodTint`. |

In `HumanService._check_add_bodyparts` and `randomizationservice.py:506-530`, eyes mode
becomes DONOTADD / ADD.

## 9. Hair (D7)

**Extend `services/haircardservice.py`** with a new `CARDS_MODE` value, `CLUMP`:

- **Generating clumps:** guide curves are grouped into clumps with the existing CLUSTER_BOX PCA
  code. Each clump becomes a tapered tube along the clump's mean curve:
  - 6 sides at the root and 3 at the tip, which also closes the tip
  - a cross-section flattened 2:1 along the PCA minor axis
  - a slight twist taken from the curve
- **Budget:** 3–6k tris per hairstyle, enforced by a `max_tris` input that merges clumps until
  the style fits.
- **Hair shader** (`nodewrapperstylizedhair.py`):
  - a root-to-tip gradient from `HairRoot` to `HairTip`, with the root 15% darker
  - an anisotropic sheen band, made with a Principled anisotropic specular set to anisotropy
    0.8 and tangent from the UV-along-strand
  - roughness 0.45
  - no alpha
- **Bake:** export bakes the shader to albedo, roughness and normal, using
  `hairbakeservice.py`.
- **Assets:** 8 default hairstyles ship as curve sets in `data/hair/` and are converted at load.

The existing alpha-card modes stay in the code, but the UI default becomes `CLUMP`.
`hairoverlayservice.py`, which bakes hair onto the skin, is kept for buzz cuts and stubble. Its
output is clamped to flat colour, with no strand-texture noise (R1).

## 10. LODs and export

Changes to `services/exportservice.py` and `ui/operations/exportops/`:

- **New export properties:**
  - `lod_count`: 1–4, default 4
  - `lod_ratio`: default 0.5
- **How LODs are made:** after `bake_modifiers_remove_helpers`, each LOD n ≥ 1 is made from LOD
  n−1 with a DECIMATE modifier (COLLAPSE) at `lod_ratio`.
  - It is weighted by a `lod_protect` vertex group shipped in `basemesh_vertex_groups.json`.
  - The group covers the eye and mouth rings, the fingers and the silhouette loops, with weight
    1.0. The `invert_vertex_group` / `vertex_group_factor` settings are tuned so these areas
    decimate last.
- **What carries over:** shape keys and weights are kept with the existing
  `_apply_modifiers_keep_shapekeys`. A LOD3 below about 3k tris drops the face shape keys.
- **Naming:** objects are named `<name>_LOD0..3` to the Unreal and Unity import conventions.
- **Clothes and hair** go through the same LOD loop, each with its own triangle count halving.

Creating the human does not decimate anything. LODs exist only in the export copy.

## 12. Optional phase: community assets

This phase is lowest priority and is done only after Phases 1–6 ship.

- **Tool:** `tools/convert/convert_mhclo.py` rewrites a `.mhclo` / `.proxy` built against HM08
  into one built against `sty01`:
  1. Fit the asset onto HM08 posed in the stylized default shape, with the existing fitting.
  2. Re-bind each asset vertex to the nearest `sty01` triangle.
  3. Write the file out with `basemesh sty01`.
- **Weights files** are re-transferred the way §5.4 transfers them.
- **Delivery:** it is a command-line tool, not a load-time hook. The loader stays strict.
- **Limitations:** converted realistic clothes keep their realistic textures, which breaks R3.
  Users are told this in the tool's output. Restyling them is out of scope.

## 14. Risks

- **Converted face targets look mushy.** HM08's face is denser than `sty01`'s, so the binding
  averages away detail. That is acceptable for a stylized look. The §5.2 hand pass covers the
  failures.
- **Mitten feet break toe animation.** Rigs with toe bones lose them (§5.4). Mixamo and
  game-engine rigs have no toe bones on most characters anyway.
- **Bust macro and clothing.** The optional converted clothes (§12) were fitted against the
  realistic breast targets. Converted tops may clip at `bust` = 1.
- **Decimated LOD3 faces.** COLLAPSE decimation can still pinch the mouth at 1.25k tris. The
  fallback is a hand-authored LOD3 head that replaces the decimated head region at export.
